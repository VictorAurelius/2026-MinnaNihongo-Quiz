## ✅ Detailed Changes

### Lesson 3: ここは ～です (Nơi này là ~)
**Added 5 supplementary words - Common Items & Phrases:**

1. **携帯電話（けいたいでんわ）** - điện thoại di động / Mobile phone
   - Example: 携帯電話は　どこですか。

2. **屋台（やたい）** - quầy hàng rong / Food stall, street vendor
   - Example: 屋台は　あそこです。

3. **いらっしゃいませ** - Xin chào quý khách / Welcome (shop greeting)
   - Example: いらっしゃいませ。

4. **見せてください** - Cho tôi xem / Please show me
   - Example: それを　見せてください。

5. **それでは** - Thế thì, vậy thì / Well then, in that case
   - Example: それでは、これを　ください。

**Result:** 58 → 63 total words (51 main + 12 supplementary)

## 💡 Rationale

These words were added as **supplementary vocabulary** because:

1. **Practical Usage:** Commonly encountered in daily Japanese life
   - 携帯電話 (mobile phone) - essential modern vocabulary
   - いらっしゃいませ - heard in every shop
   - テスト - essential for students

2. **Cultural Relevance:** Important for understanding Japanese culture
   - お疲れ様でした - essential workplace/social phrase
   - 屋台 - common feature of Japanese festivals

3. **Thematic Completeness:** Fill gaps in existing categories
   - Seafood vocabulary (鮭, 鯖, etc.) - complement existing food section
   - バドミントン - additional sport to サッカー and テニス

4. **User Request:** Explicitly requested by learner for their study needs
